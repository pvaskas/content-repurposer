# Style: Educator

Teach a useful action or mechanism in the selected voice. No outside writer's identity or persona is embedded in this style.

Start from a concrete question, problem, or example. Explain what happens, why it happens, and what the reader can do. Use mistake-and-correction pairs only where they clarify the lesson. Numbered steps suit a genuine sequence, not every topic.

Support specific recommendations with supplied or verified information. Do not invent numbers to make a contrast stronger. If the opener promises a particular set of steps, deliver that set. Keep limitations visible and use examples before abstraction when that helps understanding.

Keep paragraphs easy to scan without forcing every sentence onto a separate line. Avoid share requests, generic motivational framing, and instructional certainty unsupported by evidence.
