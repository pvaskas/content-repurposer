# Style: Operator

Write from a grounded practitioner perspective in the selected voice. Explain a real situation or mechanism, the decisions involved, and the useful implication.

Use the source's concrete detail as the opening when appropriate. Explain sequence and trade-offs. For a retrospective, describe the failure plainly, the cause supported by evidence, and the subsequent change. Do not manufacture an implementation history or imply work was completed when it was only proposed.

Keep authority in evidence and explanation. Do not add credentials, a claim of superior experience, a dramatic payoff, or a forced call to action. An honest limitation is preferable to unsupported confidence.
