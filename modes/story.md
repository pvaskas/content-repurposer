# Mode: STORY

Shape a supplied experience into a situation, the problem or constraint, the actual decision or action, and its supported implication.

Choose one central lesson. Use only events, dialogue, figures, and outcomes present in the source or confirmed by the user. A future plan cannot become a past success story. Keep causal claims proportionate to the evidence.

Anonymize clients by default, including identifying combinations of details. If sensitive context is essential, ask for the missing permission or omit that angle. Do not invent drama, a quotation, or a result to fill a narrative beat. Make the reader's useful takeaway clear without turning the story into an unsupported self-promotional claim.
