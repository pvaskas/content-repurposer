# Platform: LinkedIn

Use a composed conversational register, clear paragraphs, and one connected argument. Around 120-250 words is a useful drafting default, not a platform limit; honor the requested length.

Place the main situation or claim early so the opening makes sense on its own. Preview truncation varies; do not promise an exact number of visible lines. Preserve qualifications and source links where they are needed. Do not make unsupported claims about algorithmic reach or automatically move citations into comments.

Avoid decorative Unicode formatting, unsolicited hashtags or emojis, empty agreement questions, and a repeated conclusion. Use a call to action only when it fits the request. Split multiple substantive arguments only when the user wants multiple posts.
