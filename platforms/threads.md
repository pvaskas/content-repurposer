# Platform: Threads

Use a conversational register suited to the user's voice. Keep paragraphs readable and the core point connected. A genuine question can fit an exploratory post, but is not a mandatory ending.

Adapt the phrasing and pacing rather than copying another platform's draft unchanged. Preserve important evidence, caveats, and the user's certainty. Avoid assumptions about what the platform algorithm rewards.

Honor the requested post or thread length and verify current product limits when they matter. Do not add hashtags, emojis, or a series unless requested or supported by the brief. Drafting does not authorize posting.
